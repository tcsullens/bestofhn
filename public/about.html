Best of HN

About

Best of HN lists the most popular Hacker News posts of the last 24 hours, week, month or year, ranked by points or by number of comments, with some extra tags generated from the post title and comments.

Job posts aren't included; content is updated hourly.

This site isn't affiliated with Y Combinator or Hacker News. It's an alternative view of HN content based on my personal preferences and links back to HN where applicable. https://hn.algolia.com/ is also a worthwhile alternative.

Data

Posts and comments come from the HN Search API by Algolia. Every post links to its discussion on Hacker News.
